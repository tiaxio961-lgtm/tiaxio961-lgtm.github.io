PRACTICE YOUR NOTE-TAKING

Take notes as quickly as possible!

Try to write down the important information without writing every single word. Start with easy and work your way up.

Level 1 - Easy

My phone number is 803-555-2741. My address is 124 Green Street, Columbia, South Carolina. My appointment is Monday at 9:30 AM.

Important Notes

Phone: 803-555-2741
Address: 124 Green Street, Columbia, SC
Appointment: Monday - 9:30 AM

Level 2 - Normal

My name is Michael Johnson. My phone number is 803-555-6812. My Social Security number is 472-36-9158. My address is 215 Pine Street, Columbia, South Carolina. I need to change my appointment on Tuesday at 2:30 PM. I just want to verify one more time that my appointment was scheduled for Tuesday at 2:30 PM was change to Wednesday at 10:00 AM.

Important Notes

Name: Michael Johnson
Phone: 803-555-6812
SSN: 472-36-9158
Address: 215 Pine Street, Columbia, SC
Cancel appointment
Appointment: Tuesday - 2:30 PM

Level 3 - Hard

Help! The address is 1250 Main Street, but we're not there anymore. We are now at 1400 Assembly Street. Okay, what is your name sir, and what is the situation? Do you know the people involved? No, my name is Andrew. I don't know them. I saw these guys fighting and I tried to break it up, but they started hitting me as well. Please send help. I would like to get these people arrested for assault. Please come as soon as possible. Yes sir, the police will be on the way. Just confirming one more time, what is the address again? We are at 1400 Assembly Street now. The fight started at 1250 Main Street, but they were fighting while running across the street and we ended up over here.

Important Notes

Name: Andrew
First address: 1250 Main Street
Current address: 1400 Assembly Street
Two people were fighting
Andrew tried to break up the fight
Andrew was also hit
Wants police and arrests for assault
Police are on the way
