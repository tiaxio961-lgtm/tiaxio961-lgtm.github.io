WHY INTERPRETING MATTERS

Accuracy

A professional interpreter should deliver the complete message without adding, removing, or changing information. Accuracy helps make sure both people understand exactly what was said.

Clarity

Interpret the speaker's message in a way that is easy to understand while keeping the same meaning, tone, and intent. Clear communication helps prevent confusion and misunderstandings.

Professionalism

Stay calm, respectful, and neutral while interpreting. Professionalism is especially important in medical, legal, financial, and other situations where accurate communication can have a major impact.
