INTERPRETING BASICS

Interpreting is more than translating words. The goal is to understand the speaker's message and deliver the same complete meaning.

THE THREE BASIC STEPS

1. Listen

Listen carefully to the entire message before interpreting. Pay attention to important details, tone, names, numbers, and dates.

2. Understand

Make sure you understand what the speaker means, not just the individual words they are saying.

3. Interpret

Deliver the same complete message in the other language without changing the meaning.

REMEMBER

Listen to the complete message.

Understand what the speaker means.

Deliver the same complete message.
