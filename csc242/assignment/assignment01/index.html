The Toplane Role

as a league of legends player in the toplane role, I mostly play tank and frontline champions. and before we go any futher, I need to explain what this game is about. this game is about two teams of five player with different roles, and the goal of the game is to destroy the enemy nexus. the toplane role is a solo lane where you will be facing off in a 1v1 situation before team fight breakout. (around 15-20 mintues.) at the start you really need to win the 1v1 to spread your lead to the other team. the champion i play accelerate in this play style. i play champion like mordekaiser, mundo, darius and more.

before i started playing toplane, i was a support main. and I really did not like the fact I had no control over the game. I was always relying on my teamate to make the right calls and plays, and i was not happy with the results. that is why i became a toplane main, because i wanted to have more control over the game and be able to carry my team instead of relying on them.
